## 🎙️ Recording & Technical Requirements
- **Audio Setup:** High-fidelity microphone (clean vocal track, no background noise).
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 Full HD @ 60 FPS.
- **Subtitles:** Embedded English closed-captions (`.srt` or YouTube auto-sync).
- **Chrome Flags:** Chrome Canary 138+ with `chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano` enabled to demonstrate live sub-35ms hardware inference.
